Clear about risk

Serious tools should state what they can and cannot do.

Ali AI Bot is currently a front-end prototype. It is not connected to TradingView, MT5, a broker, a live price API, or an image-analysis model. To make the requested real analyzer, the next build needs a backend with image-capable AI, licensed/reliable market data, chart/timeframe validation, secure API handling, and testing against historical and forward data.

Win rate alone does not prove profitability. Trading costs, slippage, average win/loss, and position sizing matter. A 5-second response, a minimum 100-pip target, and a 15–30 minute hold cannot be guaranteed for every setup.

This page is for education and product prototyping, not financial advice. Never advertise unverified accuracy or guaranteed profits.
